Booking terms and privacy
Please read to the end. The tick box unlocks when you reach the bottom.

                

                  
Estimate and final price
The estimate on this page is a guide. We confirm the final price with you by WhatsApp, call or email before your visit. You pay nothing until you have agreed the price.

                  
Sending a request
Sending this form is a request, not a confirmed booking. Your booking is confirmed when we reply to confirm the date, time and price.

                  
Access and safety
Someone must let our team in, or you must share access details. Please make sure water and electricity are available, or tell us if they are not. Our team may decline or stop any task that is unsafe.

                  
Your belongings
Please put away cash, jewellery, documents and fragile items before we arrive. Tell us about delicate surfaces or items to avoid.

                  
Damage
If something is damaged during the visit, tell us within 24 hours, with photos if you can, so we can look into it.

                  
Our 24-hour promise
If we miss an area on the agreed checklist, tell us within 24 hours and we will return to fix it at no extra cost.

                  
Changes and cancellations
Tell us at least 24 hours before your visit if you need to move or cancel. Later notice may mean we cannot keep your slot.

                  
Payment
We agree the amount and how you will pay when we confirm your booking.

                  
What we do with your information
We collect your name, phone number, email, address and booking details only to arrange and carry out your cleaning, to contact you about it, and to look into any concern afterwards. We do not sell your details or share them with anyone who is not part of delivering your service. We keep booking records for as long as we need them for those purposes. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing freshnestcleaningservices@proton.me.

                  
Reviews
If you send feedback and tick the box that allows it, we may show your review on our website with the name you give.

    Booking and hours
    
            
    Are you open today?
    We're open every day, 7am to 7pm. You can send a booking request at any time, and we reply during opening hours.
    
            
    How do I book?
    Complete the estimate form and press "Send booking request". Your details reach us straight away and we contact you to confirm the final price and time. Your booking is confirmed when we reply.
    
            
    How far ahead should I book?
    Online bookings start from tomorrow. For a same-day visit, call or WhatsApp us and we'll tell you what's possible.
    
            
    Where do you work?
    Enter your city and state when you book and we'll confirm we can reach you. Prices are the same in every city and state.
    
            
    Can I reschedule or cancel?
    Yes. Message us as early as you can, ideally at least 24 hours before your visit, and we'll move it or cancel it.
    
            
    Do you clean offices, shops and building sites?
    Yes. Office cleaning is usually done in the evening or at weekends so it doesn't disturb your team. After-building cleans may need a site visit before we confirm the price.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Prices and payment
    
            
    How is my estimate worked out?
    It's built room by room: bedrooms, bathrooms, living areas, kitchen and any extras. It's adjusted for the type of clean, the size of your home and how dirty it is.
    
            
    Is the estimate the final price?
    It's a close guide, shown as a range. We confirm the final price with you before the visit, especially if you've told us about extra rooms or unusual conditions.
    
            
    Do regular cleans cost less?
    Yes. Regular plans are cheaper per visit: 5% off monthly, 10% off every two weeks and 15% off weekly.
    
            
    How does payment work?
    We agree the payment method and amount with you when we confirm your booking.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    On the day
    
            
    Do I need to be home during the clean?
    No. Many clients give us access details and go about their day. If you'd rather be there, that's fine too.
    
            
    Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
    Yes. Products and equipment are included in the price. If you prefer specific products, tell us in the special instructions.
    
            
    What should I prepare before you arrive?
    Put away valuables and cash, clear loose items from floors and worktops, tell us about pets, make sure water and electricity are available, and share any gate or estate entry instructions.
    
            
    Are your cleaners vetted?
    Yes. Our cleaners are background-checked and trained before they join a team.
    
            
    What if I have pets?
    Tell us in the booking form. We may ask you to keep pets in another room or outside while we work.
    
            
    What isn't included in a standard clean?
    Anything outside the checklist for your service is an optional extra, such as inside the oven and fridge, carpets, upholstery, tile and grout, and inside windows. High-reach windows are quoted separately.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    After the clean
    
            
    What if I'm not happy with the result?
    Contact us within 24 hours and we'll send a cleaner back to fix the issue at no extra cost.
    
            
    What if something is damaged?
    Tell us within 24 hours, with photos if you can, and we'll look into it and get back to you.
    
            
    How do I leave feedback?
    Use the form in the Reviews section. We read every message. Reviews appear on the website only if you allow it, and only after we've checked them.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Your information
    
            
    What do you do with my details?
    We use them only to arrange and carry out your cleaning and to contact you about it. We don't sell them. Read the full .
    
            
    Can you delete my details?
    Yes. Email us at freshnestcleaningservices@proton.me and we'll correct or delete them.
