First time, or forgot the password?

The first time the server starts, it prints a password in its log. Your host may show this under Logs. If you set ADMIN_PASSWORD on the server, that is the password.

Lost it? Ask whoever looks after the website to stop the server, run npm run reset-password, and start it again. The Help tab explains this once you are in.

Setup checklist

    Access details

    Your daily routine

    1. Check Overview. The orange number on the Bookings tab is the count of new requests nobody has dealt with yet.
    2. Reply to every new request during opening hours: call or WhatsApp from the booking, agree the final price and time.
    3. Update each booking: New, then Confirmed once agreed, then Completed after the visit.
    4. Check Reviews when the number shows: publish good ones, and follow up on private complaints.

    What to do when...

    A new booking arrives
    1. Go to Bookings and tap the request. New ones are marked New.
    2. Read the visit, job and estimate details. Open See the breakdown to see how the price was built.
    3. Tap WhatsApp or Call to reach the customer. Agree the final price and the time.
    4. Back in the booking, set Status to Confirmed, type the Final price, add a private note (for example the gate code), then Save changes.
    5. After the visit, set the status to Completed.
    A customer wants to move or cancel a visit
    1. Open the booking and note the new date in Private notes, or set Status to Cancelled.
    2. Message the customer to confirm. Tell them the change is saved.
    3. Cancelled bookings stay in the list so you keep a record. Use Delete booking only for mistakes and tests.
    You are fully booked, or away
    1. On Overview, tap Pause online booking. The website then tells customers to call or message you instead.
    2. Optionally add an Announcement banner under Settings, for example "Fully booked until Monday".
    3. Tap Resume online booking when you are ready.
    A customer leaves a review
    1. Open Reviews. New feedback is under Waiting.
    2. Tap Publish on website to show it, or Hide to keep it private. You can only publish reviews where the customer allowed it.
    3. Unhappy customer? Their private feedback is a chance to put it right. Contact them using the details from their booking.
    You want to show a finished job
    1. Open Our work and tap Add project.
    2. Fill in the title, service and date, add a short description, and choose a cover photo. For the before and after slider, add both photos.
    3. Tick Show this project on the website and save. Only add photos you have permission to show.
    Your phone number, email or opening hours change
    1. Open Settings, change the details, and tap Save settings.
    2. The website, the "Open now" badge and what search engines see all update straight away.
    You want to keep your data safe
    1. Open Settings, find Alerts and backups and tap Download a backup. Do this now and then, and keep the file somewhere private.
    2. The server also keeps its own daily copies. They protect against mistakes, not against losing the whole server, which is why the download matters.
    3. Photos are not in the download. Whoever looks after the server should copy the uploads folder too.
    You do not get alerts when a booking comes in
    1. Open Settings, then Alerts and backups. It shows which alert channels are on.
    2. If none are on, ask whoever looks after the server to add one: phone notifications (ntfy), Telegram or email. The README explains each.
    3. Tap Send a test alert to check it works. Meanwhile this page still shows every booking.

    Add this page to your phone

    Open this page in your phone's browser, then use the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen. You will get a one-tap icon for the admin page. Do not share your phone's saved password with anyone.
