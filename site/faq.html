Booking and hours

Are you open today?

We are open every day, 7am to 7pm. You can send a booking request at any time and we reply during opening hours.

How do I book?

Go to Book now, choose a service and your city, set your rooms, pick a date and send the request. We then contact you to confirm the final price and time. Your booking is confirmed when we reply.

How far ahead should I book?

Online bookings start from tomorrow. For a same-day visit, call or WhatsApp us and we will tell you what is possible.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Message us as early as you can, ideally at least 24 hours before your visit, and we will move it or cancel it.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need your name, phone number and address. Email is optional.

Services and locations

Which services do you offer?

Residential Cleaning, Office & Commercial Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Hostel Cleaning, Move-in & Move-out Cleaning, One-time & Regular Cleaning and Post-Construction Cleaning. See what is included in each.

Where do you work?

We clean in Ibadan, Oyo, Ogbomoso, Osogbo, Ilorin and Lagos. See our locations.

Do you work outside those cities?

Not at the moment. If you are unsure whether we reach your street, message us your area and we will tell you.

What is the difference between Residential and One-time & Regular cleaning?

Both clean your home with the same checklist. Choose One-time & Regular when you want a weekly, fortnightly or monthly schedule with a discount on every visit, or a single visit with no commitment.

Do you clean hostels and shared houses?

Yes. Hostel Cleaning is priced by the number of rooms, shared bathrooms, kitchens and corridors, so you only pay for what you have.

Prices and payment

How is my estimate worked out?

It is built room by room, then adjusted for the type of clean, the size of your home and how dirty it is. Regular plans get a discount.

Is the estimate the final price?

It is a close guide, shown as a range. We confirm the final price with you before the visit, especially if you have told us about extra rooms or unusual conditions.

Do regular cleans cost less?

Yes: 5% off each visit on a monthly plan, 10% off every two weeks and 15% off weekly.

How does payment work?

We agree the payment method and amount with you when we confirm your booking. You pay nothing when you send the request.

Is the price the same in every city?

Yes. Prices are the same in Ibadan, Oyo, Ogbomoso, Osogbo, Ilorin and Lagos.

On the day

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients give us access details and go about their day. If you would rather be there, that is fine too.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Products and equipment are included in the price. If you prefer specific products, tell us in the special instructions.

What should I prepare before you arrive?

Put away valuables and cash, clear loose items from floors and worktops, tell us about pets, make sure water and electricity are available, and share any gate or estate entry instructions.

Are your cleaners vetted?

Yes. Our cleaners are background-checked and trained before they join a team.

What is not included in a standard clean?

Anything outside the checklist for your service is an optional extra, such as inside the oven and fridge, carpets, upholstery, tile and grout, and inside windows.

After the clean

What if I am not happy with the result?

Contact us within 24 hours and we will send a cleaner back to fix the issue at no extra cost.

What if something is damaged?

Tell us within 24 hours, with photos if you can, and we will look into it and get back to you.

How do I leave feedback?

Use the form on the Reviews page. Reviews appear on the website only if you allow it, and only after we have checked them.

Your information

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to arrange and carry out your cleaning and to contact you about it. We do not sell them. Read the full booking terms and privacy notice.

Can you delete my details?

Yes. Email us at freshnestcleaningservices@proton.me and we will correct or delete them.
