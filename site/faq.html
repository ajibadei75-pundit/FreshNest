Questions, answered

Clear answers about booking, prices, fumigation, what we will and will not do, payment and privacy.

Booking

How long does booking take?

About two minutes. Pick your service and city, tell us your rooms, choose a day and time, then add your details. Typical layouts are one tap, so you only adjust what is different.

What is the quote assistant?

On the booking page you can describe your place in your own words, for example "3-bedroom flat in Ibadan, greasy kitchen, deep clean on Saturday". The assistant fills in the form for you and tells you what it assumed. You can change anything.

Does the assistant decide my price?

No. The assistant only reads your description and fills in the form. Your price always comes from our fixed room-by-room rules, and we confirm the final price with you before the visit.

How do I book?

Use the booking page. You can send the request directly to us, or send it on WhatsApp. Either way it is saved with us, and you get a confirmation email with a reference.

What happens after I book?

You get an email straight away. Our team then contacts you to confirm the final price and time, and you get a second email when it is confirmed. You can follow progress any time on the Contact page.

Can I book for today?

Online bookings start from tomorrow so our teams can prepare. For a same-day visit, call or WhatsApp us and we will tell you what is possible.

Are you open on weekends and holidays?

Yes, we are open every day. You can book online at any time, even when we are closed, and we will reply when we open.

Which cities do you cover?

Ibadan, Oyo, Ogbomoso, Osogbo, Ilorin and Lagos, at the same prices. If you are not sure we reach your street, message us your area first.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Use the tracker on the Contact page, or message us. Please tell us as early as you can, ideally at least 24 hours before the visit.

Prices

How is my price worked out?

It is built room by room: bedrooms, bathrooms, living areas, kitchen and extras. It then reflects the type of service, how large and how dirty the space is, and the booking date. The breakdown beside the form shows every factor.

Why does the date change my price?

Bookings within two days cost 10% more, weekend visits cost 5% more, and a flexible date earns 5% off. Regular plans (monthly, fortnightly, weekly) are cheaper per visit.

What else can change the price?

Pets at home, no running water or electricity on site, and upper floors reachable by stairs only. Tell us in step 4 and the estimate adjusts straight away.

Is the estimate the final price?

It is a close guide, shown as a range. We confirm the final price with you before the visit, and the visit only goes ahead once you agree.

Are prices the same in every city?

Yes. There is no city or state surcharge.

How are offices and hostels priced?

By the number of rooms and shared areas: offices by private offices, meeting rooms, toilets and open-plan space; hostels by rooms, shared bathrooms, kitchens and corridors.

Fumigation

What pests do you treat?

Cockroaches, ants and flies, bed bugs, termites, rats and mice, and mosquitoes. Choose the pests in the booking form so the price and the checklist match your problem.

How is fumigation priced?

By the rooms and areas treated, plus a treatment charge for harder pests such as termites and bed bugs, and an optional outdoor area. A severe infestation costs more than a light one.

Is fumigation safe for my family and pets?

We use approved products and give you clear safety advice. Everyone, including pets, must stay out for the time we advise, and food and water must be covered or removed first. Tell us about children, pregnant women and anyone with breathing problems when you book.

Will one visit remove the pests for good?

Many problems clear after one visit, but bed bugs, termites and rodents may need a follow-up. A follow-up is included only if it is written in our agreement, and pests can return from neighbouring properties.

Our agreement

What does "our agreement" mean?

Your booking summary and the checklist we send you together are our agreement. Everything written in them will be attended to.

What if something is not in the agreement?

Anything that is not written in the agreement will not be done. This keeps things fair and clear for both sides. If you want something added, ask us before the visit and we will confirm any change to the price.

Can I ask for something extra on the day?

Our team leader can add it if you agree it together first. It is then written on the checklist, and any extra cost is agreed before work starts.

What if something on the checklist is missed?

Tell us within 24 hours and we will return to fix it at no extra cost.

Will I get a checklist and a receipt?

Yes. We send you a checklist for your visit, and a receipt for every payment you make.

On the day

Do I need to be home?

No. Many customers share access details and go about their day. If you prefer to be there, that is fine too. For fumigation, nobody may stay inside during treatment.

Do you bring supplies and equipment?

Yes, everything is included. If you prefer specific products, say so in the special instructions.

What should I prepare?

Put away valuables, clear worktops and floors of loose items, secure pets, and make sure water and electricity are available or tell us if they are not.

Is my property safe?

Our team is vetted and trained. Tell us about delicate surfaces and anything to avoid, and we will note it on your checklist.

Payment and privacy

How does payment work?

We agree the amount and the payment method with you when we confirm your booking. Nothing is charged through the website, and you get a receipt for every payment.

How do you use my information?

Only to confirm your booking, contact you about it and carry out the service. If you use the quote assistant, the text you type is used only to fill in the form. We do not sell your information. Read the full booking terms and privacy notice for details.
